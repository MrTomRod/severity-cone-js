Alert severity cone

A three-tier traffic cone that visualises alert severity. The selected level lights its tier and every tier below it.

How to use

  • Embed a cone in a README: <img src="cone-equal.svg">
  • Levels: 0 nothing, 1 yellow, 2 orange, 3 red.

To set the level from JavaScript, inline the SVG and change its data-level:

<div id="cone"><!-- contents of cone-equal.svg --></div>
<script>
  document.querySelector('#cone svg').dataset.level = 2; // 2 orange
</script>
